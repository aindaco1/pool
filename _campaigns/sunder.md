  - type: "text"
    body: |
      mina's not a bad person, but when it really counted, she made a **terrible choice.** when it came down to angering her parents or getting her brother somewhere safe, she chose the path that would save herself. now, she has to face that choice. **blake, her brother, is gone,** and now she has to find him.
      
      *sunder* is a story about guilt. about betraying your loved one, trying to escape the utter despair of your failure until there's nothing left of you. unfortunately for mina, her despair has been given flesh. *ooey, gooey flesh…* yuck. this monster isn't just a hack n' slash beast either. it attacks our mina by **stripping her senses away.** smell, taste, sight, hearing, and finally, <u>touch</u>.
      
      this gives us a great opportunity. once mina goes blind, our diligent team of animators will take over, depicting a squiggly mina wandering in a black void, her surroundings only briefly revealed by sounds and sensations. you actually saw our test footage in the video, so **you already know it's gonna be awesome.**

      short answer: me! long answer: i'm **aidan sullivan,** and i'm a film student at the **university of new mexico** and (plan to) graduate this may. i've done sound design for both live action and animated works, and i'm a firm believer that *the monster is one of the greatest tools in storytelling.* that's why this has a monster in it, duh.
      
      i've been quietly working on this story in the background for the last few years, and now, i think it's finally ready to be made! part of what's taken me this long to get *sunder* out the door is how vulnerable this story feels for me. i've drawn from many of my personal life experiences; my fears, my insecurities, my regrets. dude, it's like… *a window into my soul, maaaan.* if you wanna peer into that window, through a grainy, fuzzy lens, then i entreat thee, nay, **i beckon thee!**
      
      none of this would be getting made if it wasn't for my incredible lead producer, **sabrina falkowsky.** on top of a long history of costuming, sabrina's brand spanking new production company, **darling film co.,** is handling everything from casting, budgeting, and even the wicked graphic designs you see before you. they're also the reason i get to put this fundraiser on the pool, thanks sabrina!
      
      **the pool** is a creation of **dust wave** to make crowdfunding easier for everyone! unlike all those other crowdfunding platforms, dust wave doesn't take any pesky fees from the campaign. this means **all the money you donate lands directly in our hands.** for those of you who haven't already heard of them, [dust wave](https://dustwave.xyz) is a filmmaking collective based in albuquerque, new mexico. thank you dust wave for letting us use the pool so we don't have to give some corporation a cut of our funds!
