      *Their Love* follows Eric and Sam from first hello to last goodbye, asking what it means to truly love.

      Most great films originate from human experience, and *Their Love* is no exception. The goal is to create a story for everyone, no matter their background, and to give viewers a way to explore heartbreak without feeling alone.

    src: /assets/images/campaigns/their-love/why-their-love.png

  - type: text
    body: |
      *Their Love* is a love letter not just to the viewer, but to the Hollywood movies that made us love going to the theater: *La La Land*, *Her*, *Blade Runner 2049*, *Lost in Translation*, and more. These are films that tugged on our hearts, but also understood how color, framing, and music can carry a universal message.

      We want to achieve that level of storytelling: to make a film that, even for a minute, can transport us back to the feeling we used to get at the theater.

      ### What the Money Supports

      We do not just want New Mexico locals to share in this community; we want the rest of the world to see it too. The team plans to submit *Their Love* to film festivals around the world and work toward earning a place among the short films considered for major awards.

      A goal that big requires hard work, dedication, a great crew, and an even greater community. As in love, nothing can be done alone.

      **Production timeline**

      - **May 26, 2026:** Fundraiser starts
      - **July 26, 2026:** Fundraiser ends
      - **Mid-August 2026:** Filming begins
      - **Late August to October 2026:** Post-production
      - **December 2026:** First screening and festival submissions begin
      - **January 2027:** Final tier rewards sent

      The most important thing to the creators, after making a story you can cherish, is making sure the hard-working crew is paid, from the AD to the PA. That is why we are aiming for a $5,000 stretch goal: to secure the locations and equipment that will make this story come to life, and to support everyone who is sacrificing time and energy to make something memorable.

      Thank you for helping bring this story to life. Even reading the fundraiser and interacting with our posts helps immensely.
