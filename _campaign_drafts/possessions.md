      ## **THE PROJECT**
      
      Ian just quit his PR job, and has a plan for how tonight will end. But when he runs into Elise, a nosy stranger on the street who won't take "no" for an answer, everything gets derailed.
      
      *POSSESSIONS* is a dark comedy short film set over a single night in a New Mexico diner. Made by an Albuquerque-based cast and crew, the film will be shot over the course of three days in January 2027 with a slated premiere in May.

      ## WHY ARE WE MAKING THIS?
  - type: "image"
    src: "/assets/images/campaigns/possessions/content-dostoevsky-20261111-185036-087649bf.png"
    alt: ""
  - type: "text"
    body: |
      You probably know someone who can't switch off their work brain. Someone who fixes everyone's problems except their own. Someone who always makes an uncomfortable joke the second a conversation gets too real. We see these patterns clear as day in the people around us. But what pattern drives you? Can you name it? Most of us can't, because from the inside, a habit doesn't feel like a habit; it just feels like us being who we are.
      
      That's what *POSSESSIONS* is about:  two people whose lives are dominated by subconscious habits like these, and the one night when each of them gets a glimpse of the other's blind spot. Ian hides behind professional language and nihilism until there's little else left underneath; Elise can't stop prying into other people's lives, whatever it has cost her. It's a film about the things we own - our job title, our comfort objects, the notebook we carry everywhere -  but it's also a film about the things that own us.
      
      Within minutes, you'll be drawing conclusions about both of them. You'll catch his evasions, her meddling, and the ways each of them dodges the moments that matter most. You'll feel how easy - and maybe even satisfying - it is to read someone else's habits. But by the time you've worked them out, the question will have turned around: if you can see all this in Ian and Elise, what would a stranger notice about you?

      ## **WHAT WILL IT LOOK LIKE?**
      
      Given we're telling such a contained story, the aesthetics have to do a lot of the heavy lifting here. Everything revolves around the diner - a warm, lit-up building standing against the dark. Full of people just being alone together.
  - type: "image"
    align: "center"
    src: "/assets/images/campaigns/possessions/content-nighthawks-20261111-220720-6f8aa476.png"
    alt: ""
  - type: "text"
    body: |
      As Ian and Elise's conversation progresses through the night - we'll watch the natural light of the outside world slowly fade away until we're left with only the pale light of the moon. Ian wraps himself in cool, muted tones - while Elise is the most colorful thing in the room.
      
      Because this film is in part about the things we own, physical objects do a lot of the talking: what's on the table, what's in the pockets, what someone can't put down. All of these things are very intentional, and have plenty to say to those who are willing to watch closely.
      
      We're going to keep you stuck in long, unbroken stretches of conversation - forcing you into the same pressurized space as Ian. You'll be at the table right with him, with nowhere else to look. Think the special diner episode of *Euphoria* meets *My Dinner with André:* it's just two people, one night, and no easy way out of an uncomfortable conversation.

      ## WHERE WILL YOUR MONEY GO?
      
      This is an all-or-nothing campaign, meaning you **will not be charged until we hit our $5,500** **goal**. Every dollar after payment processing fees will go straight to the making of the film and feeding our hard-working cast and crew.
      
      - **Equipment** - $1,775 (34%): Cameras, lenses, and lighting.
      - **Location** - $1,500 (29%): We're filming for three days in a working New Mexico diner. Our insurance policy covers the shoot, so none of your support goes to coverage.
      - **Costumes, props, & makeup** - ≈ $550 (11%): everything you'll see in the diner and on the actors.
      - **Catering** - ≈ $500 (10%): feeding all of our cast and crew for the three-day-shoot.
      - **Contingency fund** - ≈ $500 (10%): a cushion for the unexpected things that always happen on a shoot.
      - **Marketing** - ≈ $350 (7%): festival submission fees and promotional material.
      
      If anything is left over, it will go directly toward additional festival submissions!
