## Approved Boundaries

- Stripe remains the only payment processor and card-data authority.
- The Worker, `PLEDGES` KV, campaign indexes, and settlement Durable Object remain the payment/application boundaries; no double-entry ledger or second payment store was added.
- The repository remains the only media store. `_data/media-optimization-manifest.json` is deterministic and rebuildable, not an authoritative database.
- Pool remains the email audience/consent source of truth. Resend Contacts/Broadcast audience sync was not added.
- No live Stripe transaction testing was performed for this release, per owner direction.
- Ambiguous manual charge recovery remains disabled because two distinct super-admin operators are not available. Reconciliation and automated retry-safe recovery are the supported controls.
